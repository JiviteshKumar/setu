# Permission requests — DRAFTS (not sent)

These are drafts for you to review, complete and send yourself. Setu has not contacted anyone.

Before sending:

- Fill in everything in `[square brackets]`.
- Find the right recipient on each organisation's **official contact or imprint page**. The addresses aren't filled
  in here on purpose: use only what those pages list today.
- Once you have a public contact, set the same contact in `SETU_CONTACT`. It then appears in the User-Agent of every
  data request, so the organisations can recognise and reach Setu.
- Keep a copy of the replies. If a reply sets conditions (attribution wording, update frequency, no caching …),
  they have to be implemented before launch.

## 1. DAAD — International Programmes database

**Where to send:** the contact listed on the DAAD website for the "International Programmes in Germany" database, or
the general DAAD contact/imprint page if none is listed.

**Subject:** Request for permission: non-commercial reuse of International Programmes data for Indian Master's applicants

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> My name is [your name]. I am building **Setu** ([website or "not yet public"]), a free, non-commercial website
> that helps students from India find Master's programmes at **public** universities in Germany and prepare their
> applications.
>
> Setu shows course information from the DAAD International Programmes database (programme name, university,
> city, language, start dates, application deadlines, tuition and semester fees, admission requirements, support
> services, and the link to the programme page). I would like to ask for your permission to reuse this information.
>
> How the data is used:
>
> - Every course page names the DAAD database as its source, links to the original DAAD programme page and shows the
>   date the data was last checked. Students are told to confirm everything with the university before applying.
> - The data is collected with at most one request per second, with a User-Agent that identifies Setu and gives a
>   contact address. Downloaded pages are cached, and the data is refreshed at most once a week.
> - Setu is free for students and has no advertising, paid features or agent services. The data is not sold or
>   passed on to anyone.
> - Only Master's programmes at public universities are shown. Public status is taken from the HRK Hochschulkompass.
>
> Could you tell me whether this use is acceptable, and whether you have conditions (for example attribution wording,
> refresh frequency, or an official export or interface you would prefer me to use instead)? If you don't agree,
> I will remove the data.
>
> Thank you very much for the database. It is the most reliable overview Indian students have.
>
> Kind regards,
> [your name]
> [email address] · [website]

## 2. HRK — Hochschulkompass

**Where to send:** the contact listed on hochschulkompass.de (imprint or contact page).

**Subject:** Request for permission: use of the Hochschulkompass university list (hs_liste) in a free student website

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> My name is [your name]. I am building **Setu** ([website or "not yet public"]), a free, non-commercial website
> that helps students from India find Master's programmes at **public** universities in Germany.
>
> Setu uses the list of higher education institutions that Hochschulkompass offers for download
> (https://www.hochschulkompass.de/hochschulen/downloads.html). It uses the following fields:
>
> - **sponsorship (public / private / church)**, so that only public universities are shown;
> - the **university's website domain**, to match courses to universities and to check that people who answer
>   questions in our student Q&A really use an email address of a public university (the address itself is never
>   published).
>
> The file is downloaded at most once a week. The source is named and linked on our "How we verify information"
> page. Setu is free, has no advertising and doesn't pass the data on.
>
> Could you tell me whether this use is acceptable, and whether there are conditions such as a particular attribution
> wording? If you don't agree, I will stop using the list.
>
> Kind regards,
> [your name]
> [email address] · [website]
