Important update for dMAT applicants
Digital Master Test (dMAT) Status Update: Test Dates Confirmed & Registration Open
The dMAT is an additional element in the APS documentation for selected Master’s applicants.
The test dates for the upcoming sessions have been confirmed for 16 January 2027 and 13 March 2027. These sessions are particularly relevant for applicants aiming to begin their master’s studies in Germany in the Winter Semester 2027/28.
Registration is now open. Please refer to the dMAT page below for detailed explanations on the affected fields of study. For applicants falling under the specified fields, the dMAT is a mandatory requirement to obtain an APS certificate.
Reminder: Master’s applicants in affected fields may continue to submit their complete APS application documents in advance. Verification will begin upon receipt, and the final APS certificate will be issued once the dMAT score report has been verified.

Academic Evaluation Center India (APS)
Science Section of the German Embassy New Delhi
APS India verifies academic documents and educational qualifications issued by Indian educational institutions for applicants who intend to study in Germany. The APS certificate is part of the documentation generally required for the German student visa procedure.
Applicants should carefully review the applicable APS requirements before starting their application. The APS procedure does not replace the admission decision of the German higher education institution or the visa decision of the competent German mission.
